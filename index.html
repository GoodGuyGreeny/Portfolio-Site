Cool Things I Did

Audio Reactive Lighting

I made a Unity script that takes waveform and spectrum data using GetSpectrumData(), processes the audio frequency information through Fast Fourier Transform analysis with FFTWindow, and uses the processed data to create a custom audio-reactive lighting effect.

The lighting system is also gated by an in-game mechanic, allowing the visual effects to respond to both the music and gameplay.

Watch the Video

Audio Based Level Generation

I built an audio-driven level generation system for Parry Hero that analyzes a song before gameplay and converts its frequency data into level decisions.

Bass, mid, and high-frequency activity are processed over time and used to influence gameplay elements such as routes, obstacles, and platforming sections.

The goal was to make each level feel connected to the structure and energy of the music without turning the game into a strict rhythm game.
