MISSION / 001UNITY SYSTEM

Audio Reactive Lighting

Making a game world respond to the music.

A Unity script reads waveform and spectrum data with GetSpectrumData() and FFTWindow analysis to drive custom lighting effects.

An in-game mechanic gates the system, so the lighting responds to both audio and gameplay.

  • UNITY
  • AUDIO ANALYSIS
  • LIGHTING
Watch the demo↗
MISSION / 002PARRY HERO

Music-Driven Level Planning

An experiment in planning levels around music.

Parry Hero analyzes songs before gameplay, using bass, mid, and high-frequency activity to shape routes, obstacles, and platforming sections.

The goal: connect each level to the music without making it a strict rhythm game.

  • AUDIO ANALYSIS
  • LEVEL GENERATION
  • GAMEPLAY
